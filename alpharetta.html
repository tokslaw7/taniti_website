Facts about Alpharetta

City’s Population

Alpharetta is a non-capital city in the Georgia State. It has an estimated population of 67,275 as of July 1, 2024

City's Year

Alpharetta city officially incorporated on December 11, 1858. It was established as the county seat of the newly created Milton County.

State's Region

Alpharetta is located in the Northern region of Fulton County in Georgia. It's part of the larger Atlanta metropolitan area.

City's Classification

Alpharetta is classfied as Suburban city due to its blends with urban amenities and suburban feel. It is considered an Urban area.

Average Income

The average household income in Alpharetta,Georgia is $140,581.The per Capita income is approximately $72,996.

Many More ...

Alpharetta is considered an affluent city. It has a high median household income, expensive real estate, and upscale amenities
